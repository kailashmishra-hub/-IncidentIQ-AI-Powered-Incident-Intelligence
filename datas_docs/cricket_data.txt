Formats of Cricket
Test Cricket: The longest format, played over five days with two innings per team.
One Day Internationals (ODI): Limited-overs matches (usually 50 overs per side) played in one day.
Twenty20 (T20): A fast-paced format with 20 overs per side.

Origins and Global Reach
History: Cricket originated in England, with records dating back to the 16th century.
Governing Body: The International Cricket Council (ICC) is the international governing body.
Popularity: It is the second most popular sport globally, boasting 2.5 billion fans, particularly in India, Pakistan, Australia, and England.
