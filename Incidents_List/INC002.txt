Incident ID: INC002

Title: Checkout process failing for customers

Description:
Customers reported that they were unable to complete purchases on the e-commerce website.
After clicking the "Place Order" button, the system returned a 500 Internal Server Error.

Investigation:
Application logs showed that the payment gateway API was timing out during order processing.

Root Cause:
The payment gateway service was experiencing high latency and request failures.

Resolution:
The payment provider was contacted and the issue was resolved on their side.
A retry mechanism was also implemented in the checkout service.
